# Location Pages (Local SEO)

The **Build location pages** button turns a plain-English service area into a
set of unique, upload-ready landing pages — one per town — a proven local-SEO
tactic for ranking in multiple nearby places.

## How it works

1. You describe your service area in normal language, e.g.
   *"Suffolk County"*, *"NYC and Long Island"*, or a list of towns.
2. A built-in **New York gazetteer** expands recognized areas (counties,
   regions) into a concrete list of towns. Unknown place names are kept
   as-is, so a business **anywhere in the US** can simply list its own towns.
3. The generator writes one distinct landing page per town.

## What it generates

Under `site_package/areas-served/`:

- `areas-served/<town-slug>/index.html` — one unique page per town.
- A **hub page** linking to all the town pages.
- A **paste-in footer `<nav>` snippet** to link the pages site-wide.

Each page is deliberately **unique** (not thin duplicate content) — town names,
local framing, and structure vary so search engines treat them as distinct,
useful pages.

## Tips

- Keep the town list realistic — pages for places you don't actually serve
  won't help and can look spammy.
- Combine with **[[Content Creation]]** service pages and the
  **[[AI Visibility (AEO-GEO)]]** local/near-me prompts for full local coverage.
- Add your NAP consistently — the off-page audit checks for it (see
  **[[Checks Reference]]**).
